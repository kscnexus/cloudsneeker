The problem

Cloud risk grows faster than teams can review it.

Every new account, repository and deployment adds settings to check. Most teams end up with scattered tools, long spreadsheets and no clear answer to the question: what should we fix first?

Too many places to look

Cloud accounts, SaaS tenants, code, images and clusters each have their own console and their own findings.

Findings without context

A long list of issues does not show which ones an attacker could chain together to reach your data.

Audits that eat weeks

Mapping evidence to CIS, ISO 27001, PCI DSS or SOC 2 controls by hand pulls engineers off their work.

Reports nobody reads

Leadership needs a clear summary. Engineers need exact steps. One raw export serves neither.

How it works

From first connection to fewer risks.

  1. Connect read-only

    Grant read-only access with a cloud role or scoped credentials. Add repositories, images and clusters for application scans.

  2. Scan on a schedule

    Run scans on demand or on a schedule, using scan profiles to choose which checks and frameworks apply.

  3. Prioritise

    Filter by severity, framework and resource, follow Attack Paths, and ask Seeker AI what to fix first.

  4. Fix and prove it

    Send findings to Jira or Slack, track progress, and share reports with leadership and auditors.
