Cloud security assessment

A point-in-time review of your cloud accounts, combining automated checks with expert analysis of architecture, identity and network design.

What we do

  • Scope agreed accounts, regions and workloads
  • Automated scans plus manual review of the highest-risk areas
  • Attack Path analysis of exposure to sensitive data
  • Read-out session with engineering and leadership
You get

A prioritised findings report, an executive summary and a remediation roadmap.

Compliance readiness

Gap assessments against CIS Benchmarks, ISO 27001, SOC 2 and PCI DSS, so you know where you stand before an auditor looks.

What we do

  • Map your environment to the framework's controls
  • Identify gaps and the evidence each control needs
  • Plan and track remediation with your team
  • Re-assess before the audit window
You get

A gap report, a control-by-control action plan and ongoing compliance views in the platform.

Application security review

A review of how you build and ship software, from dependencies and source code to images and Infrastructure as Code.

What we do

  • Dependency, static code, container, IaC and secrets scanning
  • Triage to separate real risk from noise
  • Secure development guidance for your pipeline
  • SBOM for the applications in scope
You get

Prioritised findings with fix guidance and recommendations for your delivery pipeline.

Managed cloud security

Our team runs Cloudsneeker for you and keeps watch on your environment month after month.

What we do

  • Continuous monitoring with scheduled scans and alerts
  • Triage of new findings and remediation guidance
  • Monthly executive reporting
  • Regular review calls with your team
You get

An extension of your team that keeps posture improving and leadership informed.

Security architecture advisory

Independent advice on how to design cloud environments that are secure by default.

What we do

  • Account, identity and network architecture review
  • Guardrails and policy-as-code design
  • Review of new designs before they go live
You get

Practical architecture recommendations and reusable guardrails.

Incident readiness

Prepare for a cloud security incident before it happens.

What we do

  • Review of logging, detection and alerting coverage
  • Incident response plan and runbook review
  • Tabletop exercise based on a realistic cloud scenario
You get

A readiness assessment and concrete improvements to your response plan.

How we engage

Clear scope, clear outcomes.

  1. Discovery call

    We learn about your environment, goals and deadlines.

  2. Proposal

    You get a written scope, timeline and deliverables.

  3. Delivery

    We work with read-only access and keep you updated throughout.

  4. Hand-over

    We walk through results and leave you with a plan, or keep monitoring for you.
