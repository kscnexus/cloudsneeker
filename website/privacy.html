Privacy notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who we are

KSC Nexus Pvt Ltd, 05, VKNS Greens, Mariyappana Palya, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India (“we”, “us”) operates this website and the Cloudsneeker service. Contact: info@kscnexus.com.

This website

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage; this never leaves your device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them. Google processes that request under its own privacy policy; no other data is sent.

The contact form does not send data to us. It opens your email app, and we receive only the email you choose to send.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as name, work email, company and message.
  • Account details for users of the service, such as name, email and role.
  • Configuration and security findings produced when the service scans the systems you connect.
  • Billing and contract details (company name, billing contact, invoices), support requests, and technical logs such as sign-in times, IP addresses and the actions users take in the service (the audit log).

How we use it

  • To answer enquiries and provide proposals.
  • To provide, secure and support the service.
  • To bill for the service, keep records required by law, detect abuse and protect the security of the service. We process personal data on the basis of your consent where we ask for it, to perform our contract with your organisation, and for our legitimate use in running and securing the service, as permitted by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where it applies, the EU and UK GDPR.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the service, under contract. Our current sub-processors are Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage and content delivery) and Microsoft 365 (business email). If your organisation turns on the Seeker AI assistant, the questions you ask and the relevant findings are sent to the AI model provider your organisation configures, using your organisation's own account with that provider. Integrations you enable, such as Slack, Jira or Amazon S3, receive the data you choose to send to them. We will update this list before adding a new sub-processor.

Retention

Enquiries: up to 24 months after the last contact. Account and configuration data: for as long as your organisation has an active subscription, then deleted within 90 days of the end of the subscription unless you ask for earlier deletion. Security findings and scan results: for the subscription period, under the same 90-day rule. Audit log entries: 365 days. Billing records: 8 years, as required by Indian tax and company law. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

International transfers

The service is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1, North Virginia), and this website is served through AWS infrastructure worldwide. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, cloud credentials you provide are encrypted before storage, and each customer's data is kept separate by database row-level security. Where personal data is transferred outside India or the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards the applicable law requires, such as standard contractual clauses. Customers who need data kept in a specific region can ask us about a dedicated deployment.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data and to object to some processing. Contact info@kscnexus.com. We respond within 30 days. Our Grievance Officer is Bharath Kumar C B, info@kscnexus.com, 05, VKNS Greens, Mariyappana Palya, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. If you are in India and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We will post updates to this notice on this page and change the date above.
