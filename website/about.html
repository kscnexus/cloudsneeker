About

Security that helps teams move faster.

We believe every organisation running in the cloud deserves a clear view of its risk and practical help to reduce it.

Our mission

See more. Find risk. Stay ahead.

Cloud environments change every day. Security teams are asked to keep up with more accounts, more code and more frameworks than ever, often with the same headcount.

Cloudsneeker brings cloud posture, application security, governance and reporting into one platform, and pairs it with consultants who use it every day. The result is fewer surprises, faster fixes and evidence you can show to leadership and auditors.
