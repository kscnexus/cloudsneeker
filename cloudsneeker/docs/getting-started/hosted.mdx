The hosted service is the fastest way to use Cloudsneeker. We run the platform, apply updates, and keep the check catalog current. You connect providers and read results.

## Sign in

1. Open the sign-in link from your welcome email.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or with your identity provider if your organization has [SSO](/admin/sso) configured.
3. If you were invited to an existing organization, accept the invitation. You join with the role the inviter chose.

## First steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a provider">
    Go to **Providers** and follow the guide for your platform. See [Connect a provider](/providers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a scan">
    The first scan starts when the connection test passes. See [Run a scan](/scans/run-a-scan).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review findings">
    The overview page summarizes failed findings by severity. Drill into **Findings** for the detail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data handling

- Credentials are stored encrypted. Staff cannot read them.
- Findings, resources, and reports are isolated per organization.
- Reports you download are generated on demand and not retained after the retention window.

For questions about data residency or retention, contact [support](/support).
