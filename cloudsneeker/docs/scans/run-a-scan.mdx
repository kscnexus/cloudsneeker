title: "Run a scan"
description: "Start an on-demand scan and follow its progress"
---

A scan evaluates every applicable check against one provider. The first scan starts when a provider's connection test passes; after that you run scans on demand or on a [schedule](/scans/schedules).

## Start a scan

1. Go to **Scans** and select **Launch scan**.
2. Choose the provider. Optionally give the scan a name so it is easy to find later.
3. Select **Start**.

The scan appears in the list with its progress. Duration depends on the number of resources: a small account finishes in minutes, a large one can take an hour or more.

## What a scan does

- Lists resources in every enabled region or location
- Runs every check that applies to the provider type
- Records a finding per resource per check with status `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `MANUAL`
- Compares with the previous scan to mark findings as new, unchanged, or resolved
- Generates the downloadable [reports](/scans/reports) and runs any configured [integrations](/integrations)

Scans are read-only. Nothing in the account is changed.

## Scan states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `available` | Queued, waiting for a worker |
| `scheduled` | Created by the daily schedule, waiting for its time |
| `executing` | Running; the progress bar shows checks completed |
| `completed` | Finished; findings and reports are ready |
| `failed` | Stopped early; open the scan for the error. Usually a credential or permission problem |
| `cancelled` | Stopped by a user |

## Limit a scan

Open the provider's settings to exclude regions, services, or individual checks. Excluded checks do not produce findings and do not count toward compliance scores.
