**Cloudsneeker** scans your cloud environments against thousands of security checks, maps the results to compliance frameworks, and tells you what to fix first. It runs as a hosted service operated by us, or inside your own infrastructure.

## How it works

1. **Connect** a provider with read-only credentials. Credentials are encrypted at rest and only used to run scans.
2. **Scan** on demand or on a schedule. Each scan evaluates every applicable check against every resource it can see.
3. **Review** findings by severity, service, resource, or compliance framework. Mute what is accepted risk.
4. **Export** reports as CSV, JSON, HTML, or PDF, or push findings to Amazon S3, AWS Security Hub, Jira, and Slack.
